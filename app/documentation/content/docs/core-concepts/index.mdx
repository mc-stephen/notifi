## Notifications

A notification is a message delivered to a recipient through one or more channels.

## Recipients

Recipients are the end users or systems that receive notifications.

## Templates

Templates define reusable message formats with dynamic variables.

## Events

Events trigger notifications based on conditions you define.

## Scheduling

Schedule notifications for future delivery or recurring intervals.
